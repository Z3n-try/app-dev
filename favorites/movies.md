# My Favorite Movies

1. **Inception (2010)**: A thief who enters people's dreams is given the job of planting an idea in someone's mind.
2. **Interstellar (2014)**: A group of astronauts travels through a wormhole to look for a new home for humanity.
3. **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)**: Miles Morales meets Spider-People from other universes and learns to become a hero.
4. **The Lion King (1994)**: A young lion named Simba must face his past and take his place as king.
5. **Your Name (2016)**: Two teenagers who have never met mysteriously start swapping bodies.
